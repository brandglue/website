**Is AI-generated social media copy actually hurting engagement? We ran our own two-month experiment to find out. Using identical webinar topics, the same audience, and the same posting cadence, BrandGlue compared AI-written posts against human-written posts. The results surprised us: human-written content achieved a modestly higher engagement rate (3.98% vs. 3.43%), but the gap was much smaller than expected. Here's what the data means for marketers and how we think brands should approach AI in their social media strategy going forward.**
